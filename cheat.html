Euchre Cheat Card

LaddersDrillPrint

From the r/Euchre Discord, #strategy-discussion, October 2025 – November 2026. sim: a simulation backs it. experts: the experts agree, never simulated. Ordinary scores unless a line says otherwise.

Seat 1 · Round 1

  • simOrder rarely: about 1 hand in 16.
  • simK-Q-10 of trump, no bower, no ace: order. Pass it at 8-8 and 9-8.
  • simRight + queen with an off K or A: pass. Order at 9-9.
  • simLeft-K-Q with the ace turned up: pass, then call red in round 2.
  • simLeft-A-K of trump + an off K-x: go alone.
  • simBird call (ordering junk to stop the dealer) only when they're on 9.

Seat 1 · Round 2

  • simCall about 3 hands in 4.
  • simNever call next with zero trump.
  • simJunk: call the reverse suit where you hold two trump over one-trump next.
  • simA-K of next + an off ace, no bower: pass. Call at 9-9.
  • expertsTwo suits about equal: pass. Call when one stands out.

Seat 2 (dealer's partner) · Round 1

  • simTwo trump + an off ace: order, even partner's jack. Pass only if you already cover every suit.
  • simTwo small trump that block nothing: order.
  • simLone right + three off aces: pass both rounds.
  • simAfter ordering: trump in low on trick 1, then cash your off ace before the left.
  • expertsDealer undertrumps or throws an ace on trick 1: lead trump at once.

Seat 2 · Round 2

  • simA doubleton in the likely trump suit is enough defence to pass.
  • simFour-suited support hand: stick the dealer.

Seat 3 · Round 1

  • simOrder rarely: your side wins by euchring.
  • simThree trump against a turned-up jack: pass.
  • simThree trump, two-suited, doubleton not in next: order. Pass with a green jack.
  • simDon't donate, except far ahead (7-2 or better) or at 8-6 and 8-5.

Seat 3 · Round 2

  • simThree green trump: call. Don't stick the dealer.

Dealer · Round 1

  • expertsThree small trump: pick up.
  • expertsLeft + two small: pick up, unless you're strong in next.
  • simTwo small + one off ace: pass. Pick up at 9-8 or when they're on 9.
  • simTwo trump + A-K-10 of a side suit: pick up when that suit is reverse.
  • simRight, left, A-x + another ace, jack up: go alone.
  • simAlone without the right: keep two off aces, not A-K of one suit.

Dealer · Round 2 (stuck)

  • simA-Q of next beats A-K of reverse.

Loners, any seat

  • simThree trump with the right: alone with an off ace, or A-K of trump + an off K-Q.
  • simAsk whether partner helps, not whether you'll be euchred.

Leads

  • simPartner made it and hasn't led trump: when you get in, lead trump.
  • simSeat 1, partner ordered from seat 3: lead trump. One trump + four of a suit: lead the suit.
  • simYou'll get back in: lead a side king, save your ace.
  • simThin maker on lead: lead low from an off K-x.
  • expertsThe maker has used a trump: lead something else.

Score

  • sim9-9: make the thin call.
  • simThey're on 9: thin calls and bird calls gain.
  • expertsYou're 9-8: pass thin calls. A euchre loses the game.
  • simYou're 6-8: pass marginal calls.
  • simSwingy hand: call when ahead, pass when behind.

Seat 1 is left of the dealer, seat 2 the dealer's partner, seat 3 right of the dealer. Next is the other suit of the turned-down colour; reverse (green) are the two suits of the other colour.
