Euchre Cheat Card

LearnDrillPrint

From the r/Euchre Discord, #strategy-discussion, October 2025 – November 2026. sim: a simulation backs it. experts: the experts agree, never simulated. Ordinary scores unless a line says otherwise.

Seat 1 · Round 1

  • simPass almost always.
  • simOrder when you'd go alone.
  • simOrder lighter when they're on 9.

Seat 1 · Round 2

  • simCall about 3 hands in 4.
  • simNever call next with zero trump.

Seat 2 · Round 1

  • simTwo trump + an off ace: order.

Seat 3 · Round 1

  • simOrder rarely: your side wins by euchring.
  • simThree trump against a turned-up jack: pass.

Dealer · Round 1

  • expertsThree small trump: pick up.

Loners

  • simAsk whether partner helps, not whether you'll be euchred.

Leads

  • simPartner ordered from seat 3: lead trump.
  • simYou'll get back in: lead a side king, save your ace.

Score

  • sim9-9: make the thin call.

Next is the other suit of the turned-down color; reverse (green) are the two suits of the other color.
