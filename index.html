Terminology

S1–S4: S1 left of dealer (leads trick 1), S2 dealer's partner, S3 right of dealer, S4 dealer.

R1 / R2: round 1 (order or pick up the upcard), round 2 (name a suit).

Next: the other suit of the upcard's colour. Green / reverse: the two suits of the other colour.

Ruff: trump a trick because you can't follow suit.

Stick: in stick-the-dealer, pass so the dealer must call.

Bird call: ordering a junk hand in R1 to stop the dealer picking up, mainly at 9-9.

Soft donate: a call you expect to lose, made to block a loner, with some chance to make.

Undertrump: play a smaller trump under partner's winning trump, a "no losers" signal.
