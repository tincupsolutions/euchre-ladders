Euchre Drill

LaddersCheat card

Real hands from the r/Euchre Discord. Make your call, then see what the sim or the experts say. Hands you miss come back sooner; hands you get right fade out. Many hands belong to a variant set (the same type of hand with one thing changed, or the same hand at another score); play them back to back and watch the call move. If an answer looks wrong or you have a question, flag it (⚑) and send your flags to Michele.

Answers come from seaeagle's simulations where a hand was simulated, otherwise from experts who agreed. Your progress and flags are kept in this browser only.
